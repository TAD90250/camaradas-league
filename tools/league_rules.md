# Fees and prize money
Each team pays a $100 league fee, plus $71 for the weekly prizes ($852 across the 12 teams).
## Season-end prizes
- 1st place: $760
- 2nd place: $260
- 3rd place: $120
- 1st in the regular-season standings: $100
- Most points scored in the regular season: $100
## Penalties
- The two lowest scorers of each regular-season week each pay $5 (playoff weeks do not count).
- A hurt player or a player on a bye left in your lineup costs $20. The fee is added to the 1st place prize.
- A team that misses the playoffs pays $10. Those payments build a $60 pot that goes to the toilet bowl winner. The initial league fee does not go into the toilet bowl pot.
- Last place in the regular-season standings (including Yahoo's tie-breakers) is the beer bitch at the next draft and must wear the cheerleader outfit. Instead of wearing the outfit (the beer bitch duties still apply), they can pay $1,100, which goes to the other 11 players as $100 each toward next year's league fee. If the last-place team wins the toilet bowl, the 11th-place team takes over the outfit and the beer bitch duties.

# Weekly payouts
The weekly prizes are paid from the extra $71 per team. The schedule below is the same one shown on the Payouts page.
- Last team to lose its undefeated record: $52. If teams lose their last unbeaten game in the same week, the tied teams keep going until one loses and the other does not; the one that does not lose wins.
- Week 1: highest score, $50. Tie-breaker: the team with the highest-scoring player.
- Week 2: the team that started the highest-scoring player who is not a QB, $50.
- Week 3: the team with the highest-scoring starting TE, $50.
- Week 4: the team with the most touchdowns from its starting lineup (passing touchdowns included), $50. A tie-breaker extends to the next week.
- Week 5: the team that beat the losing team with the highest score of the week, $50.
- Week 6: highest score, $50.
- After week 7: the team in 1st place in the standings, $100.
- Week 8: the biggest blowout, $50.
- Week 9: the highest-scoring starting kicker, $50.
- Week 10: the highest-scoring starting QB, $50.
- Week 11: the highest-scoring starting WR, $50.
- Week 12: the highest-scoring starting RB, $50.
- Week 13: the highest-scoring starting DST, $50.
- End of week 14: the team with the top-scoring non-QB player of the season so far, $50.
- End of week 14: the team that beat the best-ranked loser of the week, $50.

# Keepers
- We draft normally through the end of round 4 without selecting a keeper. Then the draft pauses and keepers are decided.
- If you keep a player, you lose the draft round that player was selected in the year before (even if you did not draft him).
- If he was selected before round 5 the previous year, you lose your round 5 pick. If he was selected after round 10, you lose your round 10 pick.
- A player can only be kept if he was not a keeper for any team the previous year. You cannot keep a player who was kept in the previous draft.
- To keep a player, he must have been on your roster when the previous season ended.
- After week 13 ends (Monday night), if you pick up a player and do not use him for the rest of the season, he does not count as a keeper option for the next draft. Only the real playoff games count as season games; the toilet bowl, 3rd place and 5th place games do not.

# Trades
- Once two managers accept a trade, the rest of the league has 24 hours to make a better deal with one of those managers and convince them to trade with them instead.
- A newly accepted trade does not restart the 24-hour clock. If it is accepted in the 20th hour or later, 2 hours are added.
- To cancel the original trade, the new trade must include the top-ranked player on Sleeper's rest-of-season projections.
- FAAB in trades: an official counter must be an increment of at least $5 of FAAB. If you have less than that, your maximum available is the counter.
- The initial trade must be submitted by Saturday 4pm or it is cancelled. That leaves enough time for counters. No counter offers after 11:59 PM Saturday, and the trade can be completed right after.
- Please remind the commissioner when a trade has been accepted, and when the 24 hours have passed.

# Rosters and the draft
- The draft is 16 rounds. After week 3, rosters drop to 14 active spots (the 1 IR spot stays).
- Teams must cut down to 14 active players and 1 IR by 10PM Monday night of week 3. If a team misses the deadline, the system locks the team until the players are dropped, so dropping players late is allowed.
- For weeks 12 to 14, a lottery on draft day decides the matchups so that matchups from weeks 1 to 3 are not repeated. The lottery decides which weeks get repeated.

# Playoffs and FAAB
- After the last regular-season game, all remaining FAAB is removed from teams that did not make the playoffs.
- Each remaining playoff team then receives an additional $30 of FAAB, to give playoff teams more chances to land a player during the real playoffs.
