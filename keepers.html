Keeper rules

  • The draft runs normally through round 4, then pauses so keepers can be declared.
  • Keeping a player costs the round he was drafted the year before, even if you did not draft him. Drafted before round 5 (rounds 1–4): you lose your 5th. Drafted after round 10, or not drafted: you lose your 10th.
  • A player must have been on your roster when the season ended.
  • A player who was a keeper in the previous draft cannot be kept again.
  • After week 13, a player you pick up only counts if he starts for you in a real playoff game. The 3rd place, toilet bowl and other consolation games do not count.
